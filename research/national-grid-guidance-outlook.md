# National Grid plc (LSE: NG. / NYSE: NGG) — Management Guidance & Outlook

**Compiled: 8 July 2026** | Fiscal year ends 31 March
**Sources covered:** FY2026 full-year results & annual report (14 May 2026 / June 2026), H1 FY2026 half-year results (6 Nov 2025), FY2025 full-year results (15 May 2025), plus the RIIO-T3 Final Determination response (4 Dec 2025) and the Upgraded 5-Year Framework / RIIO-T3 acceptance announcement (2 Mar 2026).

---

## 1. FY2026 Full-Year Results — 14 May 2026 (latest call; matches the FY26 Annual Report)

### Reported FY26 (year ended 31 March 2026)
- Underlying EPS **78.0p, +8%** at constant currency (strong operating performance partially offset by divestments, storm costs, higher share count, and a recent FERC order).
- Record capital investment of **£11.6bn** (FY25: £9.8bn), driving group asset growth of **10.9%**.
- Underlying operating profit **£5.7bn, +9%**, driven by higher allowed revenues in UK Electricity Transmission.
- Group Return on Equity **9.8%** (+80bps), principally from increased regulated revenue in UK ET and New York.
- Net debt **£44.2bn** (+£2.8bn); regulatory gearing **61%**.
- Total dividend **48.49p, +3.8%** (final 32.14p) — in line with the policy of growing DPS with UK CPIH inflation.

### Guidance for 2026/27 (FY27)
- **Underlying EPS growth of 13–15%**, reflecting higher allowed revenue as delivery steps up from RIIO-T2 to RIIO-T3.
- UK Electricity Transmission underlying net revenue expected to increase by **just under £850m** vs 2025/26 — first year of RIIO-T3, semi-nominal returns and higher totex from increased ASTI investment.
- Depreciation expected **~£80m higher**; costs expected **~£50m higher**, both linked to the step-up in investment programmes.
- Group Return on Equity expected to be **around 9%** in 2026/27.
- Long-run average **scrip dividend uptake assumption of 25% p.a.**, supporting a sustainable, progressive dividend.

### New Five-Year Financial Framework to 2030/31 (extended & upgraded)
- **At least £70bn capital investment** over FY27–FY31 (vs the prior ~£60bn FY25–FY29 plan), largely backed by regulatory agreements in the UK and US.
- Group **asset growth ~10% CAGR**; group assets expected to reach **~£115bn by FY31**.
- **Underlying EPS CAGR of 8–10%** from the 2025/26 baseline of 78.0p (upgraded from 6–8%) — "more aligned with asset growth".
- Capex allocation: **UK Electricity Transmission ~£31bn** (a 150% increase on the prior five years; connecting up to 35GW of generation and 19GW of new demand), **UK Electricity Distribution ~£9bn**, **New York ~£17bn**, **New England ~£12bn**, **National Grid Ventures ~£1bn**.
- Around **two-thirds of the programme already covered by regulatory agreements**; **~three-quarters of delivery mechanisms secured**.
- Dividend: maintain a progressive total dividend, growing **DPS in line with UK CPIH**.
- Balance sheet: committed to a **strong, overall investment-grade credit rating**; regulatory gearing expected to **trend back to the high-60% range by 2030/31** (from 61%), with balance sheet strength extending beyond 2030/31 and **significant hybrid capacity**; no new ordinary equity flagged beyond scrip.

### Regulatory outlook given with FY26 results
- **RIIO-T3 (UK ET, Apr 2026–Mar 2031):** accepted in full; management confident of delivering an **overall return on equity above 9%** across the price control (including operating and financing performance) against Ofgem's allowed real cost of equity of **6.12% at 60% gearing**. CEO John Pettigrew: focus was on the "overall investable framework" and "workability", with the view that base returns should be internationally comparable.
- **New York:** Niagara Mohawk (upstate) three-year rate plan approved — a key source of framework visibility. NY achieved RoE of 9.0% in FY26 (96% of allowed, up from 8.7%).
- **Massachusetts:** ESMP (Electric Sector Modernization Plan) investments approved.
- **FERC:** March 2026 order on the four New England Transmission Owners base-ROE complaints set a **9.57% base ROE** with refunds (with interest). National Grid is challenging the decision and, with the other NETOs, filed under FPA Section 205 in April 2026 proposing a **forward-looking base ROE of 11.39%**.
- CFO Andrew Agg: currency and equity-issuance impacts were "more than offset by improved operating performance in the regulated businesses and slightly lower financing costs".

### Market reaction
Shares fell ~7% on results day despite the beat — investor concerns centred on the step-up in capex, rising net debt, the gearing trajectory back to high-60s%, and scrip-driven dilution.

## 2. Interim announcements between calls

### Ofgem RIIO-T3 Final Determination — response (4 Dec 2025)
- Ofgem's Final Determination set a real allowed cost of equity of **6.12% at 60% gearing** (an improvement on the Draft Determination but below National Grid's request). Ofgem approved £28.1bn of upfront investment across the RIIO-3 electricity price controls (+16% vs draft), with a wider signalled pipeline of ~£90bn sector-wide.

### Upgraded 5-Year Framework & RIIO-T3 acceptance (2 Mar 2026)
- National Grid **accepted all RIIO-T3 price control arrangements** for UK ET (April 2026–March 2031).
- Framework upgraded ahead of the FY26 results: **≥£70bn capex to FY31**, **~10% asset growth**, **EPS CAGR upgraded to 8–10%**, assets **~£115bn by FY31**, and confidence in **overall RoE above 9%** across the price control.

## 3. H1 FY2026 Half-Year Results — 6 Nov 2025

### Reported H1 (Apr–Sep 2025)
- Underlying operating profit **£2,292m, +13%** at constant currency — driven by new Niagara Mohawk rates in New York, rate increases and storm recoveries at Massachusetts Electric, higher UK revenues from increased investment, and Grain LNG's reclassification as held-for-sale (depreciation ceased).
- Record H1 capital investment of **£5bn**; on track for **>£11bn** for the full year.

### Guidance given at H1 (for FY26)
- Underlying EPS growth **in line with the 6–8% CAGR** range from the FY25 baseline of 73.3p (this was subsequently beaten — FY26 delivered +8%, and full-year EPS guidance was upgraded during H2).
- Capital investment **>£11bn**; group asset growth **~11%**; net debt to increase **~£1.5bn** from 31 March 2025.
- DPS to grow in line with **UK CPIH**.

### Framework then in force (FY25–FY29)
- Cumulative capex **~£60bn**; group asset growth **~10% CAGR**; underlying EPS CAGR **6–8%** from the FY25 baseline of 73.3p.

### Portfolio & regulatory outlook at H1
- **Grain LNG** sale agreed for **£1.66bn**, completion expected before end of 2025 (part of the pivot to networks; follows the ESO and National Gas Transmission exits, with National Grid Renewables also being sold).
- Awaiting Ofgem's RIIO-T3 Final Determination (due Dec 2025).

## 4. FY2025 Full-Year Results — 15 May 2025

### Reported FY25 (year ended 31 March 2025)
- Underlying EPS **73.3p, +2%**, slightly ahead of guidance — underlying operating profit growth more than offset the enlarged share count from the 2024 £7bn Rights Issue.
- Record capital investment of **£9.8bn**; regulated asset growth **10.5%**.
- Regulatory gearing reduced to **61%** (from 69%), helped by the Rights Issue, £0.7bn from the sale of the final 20% of National Gas Transmission, and £0.6bn from the ESO disposal.
- Total dividend **46.72p, +3.2%** on the rebased base.

### Guidance given at FY25 (for FY26 and the 5-year plan)
- FY26 underlying EPS growth in line with the **6–8% CAGR** from the 73.3p baseline; guidance assumed completion of the ESO, Grain LNG and National Grid Renewables disposals.
- Five-year plan (FY25–FY29): **~£60bn** capital investment, **~10%** asset growth CAGR, **6–8%** underlying EPS CAGR; supply chain and delivery mechanisms secured for **more than two-thirds** of the plan.
- Dividend policy: grow DPS in line with **UK CPIH**.

## 5. How the guidance has evolved (summary)

| Metric | FY25 call (May 2025) | H1 FY26 (Nov 2025) | FY26 call (May 2026) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5-yr capex | ~£60bn (FY25–29) | ~£60bn (FY25–29) | **≥£70bn (FY27–31)** |
| Asset growth CAGR | ~10% | ~10% | ~10% (assets ~£115bn by FY31) |
| Underlying EPS CAGR | 6–8% (base 73.3p) | 6–8% (base 73.3p) | **8–10% (base 78.0p)** |
| Next-year EPS | 6–8% growth | in line with 6–8% | **+13–15% for FY27** |
| Dividend | DPS grows with UK CPIH | unchanged | unchanged (scrip uptake assumed 25%) |
| Gearing | 61% post-rights-issue | net debt +~£1.5bn in FY26 | trending to high-60s% by FY31 |
| Credit | strong investment grade | unchanged | unchanged, plus significant hybrid capacity |

**Direction of travel:** each event has upgraded the story — the RIIO-T3 settlement (accepted March 2026) unlocked a larger, longer framework (≥£70bn to FY31), a step-change FY27 (EPS +13–15%), and a higher through-cycle EPS CAGR (8–10%), funded without new ordinary equity (scrip + hybrids), at the cost of gearing drifting back toward the high-60s%. Key watch items management flagged: the FERC NETO base-ROE challenge (9.57% ordered vs 11.39% requested), execution of the ~£31bn UK ET/ASTI programme, and the New York/New England rate calendars.
