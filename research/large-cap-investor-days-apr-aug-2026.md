# Large-Cap Capital Markets Days & Investor Days — April–August 2026

**Compiled: 6 August 2026**
**Screen:** companies with market cap ≥ **USD 20bn** that held a Capital Markets Day (CMD) / Investor Day / Analyst Day in the **last 4 months (~6 Apr – 6 Aug 2026)**.
**Focus:** which of these delivered an *improved* outlook (upgraded targets, raised capital returns, better-than-expected framework) versus in-line or disappointing.

Market caps are approximate, quoted around the event date, and converted at spot.

## 2. Improved outlooks — the ones to focus on

### Equinor (OSE: EQNR / NYSE: EQNR) — CMD, 16 June 2026
The clearest positive surprise in the window; the upgrade was on capital returns *and* volumes.
- **Buyback doubled** for 2026 to **USD 3bn** (from USD 1.5bn), the increase split evenly across the third and fourth tranches.
- New **range-based buyback framework of USD 2–4bn p.a. from 2027**, framed against Brent $60–80/bbl and European gas $7–11/mmbtu — a more predictable, more visible return profile than the prior ad-hoc approach.
- **Dividend:** quarterly cash DPS to grow **>5% annually**.
- **Production:** +150 kboe/d to **2.3 mboe/d by 2030**; NCS +100 kboe/d to **1.35 mboe/d**.
- **Cash flow:** CFFO after tax **+30% (2025–2030)**; **FCF >USD 40bn cumulative 2026–2030**.
- **Power:** output to **>20 TWh by 2030**.
- *Why it matters:* the FCF guide of >$40bn compares with Visible Alpha consensus of ~$29bn and Jefferies' own ~$32bn — i.e. a materially above-consensus number, with the buyback hike and Norway volumes both landing ahead of preview expectations.

### BE Semiconductor Industries — Besi (AMS: BESI) — Investor Day, 18 June 2026
The only name in the cohort that formally **raised** an existing long-term target model.
- **Long-term revenue target raised to €1.7–2.2bn** (from €1.5–1.9bn).
- **Operating margin floor raised to 45%** (from 40%); top of range unchanged at 55%.
- Driver: order momentum plus demand for **2.5D AI datacenter and photonics** applications, and new hybrid-bonding use cases.
- No timeframe attached to the targets — the main soft spot. Stock had already roughly doubled YTD into the event, so much is priced.

### Dexcom (NASDAQ: DXCM) — Investor Day, 14 May 2026
- **Organic revenue growth of 10%+ every year through 2030.**
- **2030 non-GAAP gross margin 67–69%**; **non-GAAP operating margin 29–30%**; **adjusted EBITDA margin 36–37%**.
- **New $1.0bn buyback** authorised (through 30 Jun 2027), replacing a prior programme with $250m remaining.
- Stock rose ~7%; RBC kept Outperform ($85 PT) and characterised the long-range plan as **conservative** — i.e. the guide is seen as beatable.

### Hexagon AB (STO: HEXA-B) — CMD, 30 April 2026, London
New 2026–2030 framework, and a genuine step-up versus what the company actually delivered in 2025.
- **Organic revenue growth 4–6%** (vs **2.6% delivered in 2025**; 2019–24 historic average ~4%).
- **EBITAC margin 24–26%** (vs **22% in 2025**).
- **EBITAC cash conversion 90–100%** (vs 109% in 2025 — the one metric guided *below* the current run-rate).
- Introduced **EBITAC** as the key profitability metric, with revised metric definitions aimed at transparency and capital allocation.
- CEO Anders Svensson positioning: a "focused global leader in precision measurement and positioning… essential to enabling industrial autonomy."
- *Read:* margin and growth ambition are the upgrade; the cash-conversion guide is a mild offset, and the metric change makes clean like-for-like comparison harder.

### Capgemini (EPA: CAP) — CMD, 27 May 2026
Agentic-AI-framed strategy with a concrete margin build.
- **2025–28 revenue CAGR +5.5% to +7.5%** at constant currency, of which **~2pts from M&A**.
- **Operating margin +130–150bps between 2025 and 2028, to 12.1–12.3%** — the core upgrade.
- **Cumulative organic FCF >€6bn over 2026–2028.**
- **2026 guidance:** revenue growth **+6.5% to +8.5%** cc (inorganic ~4.5–5pts); organic FCF **€1.8–1.9bn**.
- *Caveat:* a large share of headline growth is acquisition-funded — organic is the number to watch.

### DSV A/S (CPH: DSV) — CMD, 12 May 2026, Hedehusene
"Leverage to Lead" — new 2030 targets, and importantly an **incremental** profit pool rather than a re-cut of the existing one.
- **DKK 9bn annual productivity savings by 2030**: ~**DKK 6bn from AI and technology**, ~**DKK 3bn from network/operations optimisation**.
- This is **on top of unchanged Schenker integration synergies of ~DKK 9bn**, with full financial impact from 2027 — management framed the AI opportunity as capable of delivering "another Schenker-sized gain".
- **FY2026 guidance reconfirmed:** EBIT before special items **DKK 23–25.5bn**.
- Capital allocation policy unchanged: gearing **<2.0x EBITDA** (NIBD incl. leases).

## 3. New frameworks — credible but not an upgrade against a like-for-like base

### Honeywell Technologies (NASDAQ: HON) — Investor Day, 11 June 2026, NYC
Held ahead of the **29 June 2026 spin-off of Honeywell Aerospace**, so this is a re-basing of the RemainCo, not a raise.
- **Organic revenue growth 4–6% annually.**
- **Operating margin expansion >60bps every year.**
- **Earnings growth >10% annually.**
- **Free cash flow conversion 90%+.**
- Presented by Chairman & CEO Vimal Kapur. Treat the three-year framework as the new baseline against which post-spin execution is judged.

### Stellantis (NYSE: STLA) — Investor Day, 21 May 2026
"FaSTLAne 2030" — a recovery plan off a heavily depressed base; ambitious in direction, but the targets restore rather than exceed prior profitability.
- **Revenue €154bn (2025) → €190bn by 2030.**
- **AOI margin 7% by 2030.**
- **Industrial FCF positive in 2027, rising to €6bn in 2030.**
- **Cost reduction run-rate €6bn by 2028** vs 2025, growing further to 2030.
- **Stellantis Financial Services AOI >€1.5bn in 2030.**
- Plan spend variously reported at €60bn / ~$70bn over five years.
- *Read:* the 7% 2030 margin is well below the ~10%+ Stellantis printed in its 2022–23 peak years — this is a normalisation story, and the back-end loading (FCF only positive in 2027) leaves a long show-me period.

### Tyler Technologies (NYSE: TYL) — Investor Day, 9 June 2026, Frisco TX
- Updated long-term financial targets and capital allocation framework supporting the **2030 vision**; themes were the next phase of SaaS growth, the transactions/payments platform, and AI strategy.
- Morningstar's read: **"slightly higher 2030 targets"** and a steady vision — an incremental positive rather than a re-rating event. BTIG said the day reinforced its view of a "much stronger company."

## 4. Disappointment

### Citigroup (NYSE: C) — Investor Day, May 2026
The largest company in the cohort, and the one that went the wrong way.
- **ROTCE 11–13% (ex-notables) for 2027 and 2028**, expected toward the higher end in 2028.
- **Medium-term ROTCE 14–15% for 2029–2031.**
- **2026 ROTCE target maintained at 10–11%** (2025 actual: 8.8%).
- **$30bn share buyback** announced alongside.
- **Shares fell on the day.** The trajectory is upward, but the near-term commitment landed below what the market had positioned for — the 14–15% is pushed out to the end of the decade, and the $30bn buyback did not offset it.

## 5. Just outside the window / borderline — worth keeping on the radar

- **Quanta Services (NYSE: PWR)**, ~$60bn — Investor Day **31 March 2026** (six days outside the screen). Laid out a path to **more than doubling adjusted EPS by 2030**, against a **$2.4trn TAM through 2030** across utility, generation and large-load markets. Arguably the most aggressive long-term guide of the broader period.
- **Securitas (STO: SECU-B)**, ~SEK 90bn (~**$9.5bn** — below threshold) — CMD **15–16 June 2026**, London. New headline target of **10% average annual EPS growth** over a cycle, 4–6% organic growth, long-term 10% operating margin ambition, operating cash flow 80–90% of EBITA, ND/EBITDA <2.5x, payout 50–60%. Shares fell ~2.5–3.2% on a "sell the news" reaction. Included because the target set is a genuine upgrade even though the market cap misses the screen.
- **Legrand**, ~$40bn — CMD scheduled **29 September 2026** in Singapore (2030 framework: €15bn sales, >20% adjusted operating margin, ~€10bn cumulative FCF 2025–30). Upcoming, not in window.

## 6. Adjacent — outlook upgrades that came at results, not at an investor day

Flagged separately because they do not meet the CMD/Investor Day definition, but they are large-cap outlook improvements in the same window:
- **Barclays** — 2026 Group income target upgraded to **~£31.5bn**; all 2026 and 2028 financial and distribution targets reaffirmed.
- **Sanofi** — Q2 (30 Jul) guidance upgraded; Dupixent >€5bn in a single quarter and pointed to **~€25bn Dupixent sales in 2030** plus ~€10bn from pharma launches.
- **GSK** — Q2: stable-to-improving operating margins through the dolutegravir LOE period (2028–30), a meaningful improvement on prior guidance; **>£40bn sales ambition by 2031** reiterated.
- **Pfizer** — raised the midpoint of 2026 revenue guidance to **$60.5–62.5bn**.
- **Schneider Electric** — upgraded its 2026 target on Electrification/Automation demand and a record backlog.

## 7. Screening take

- **Highest-conviction upgrades:** Equinor (capital returns + above-consensus FCF), Besi (formal target raise, though heavily pre-priced), Dexcom (guide described as conservative by the sell side).
- **Second tier, execution-dependent:** Hexagon (margin ambition vs a 2.6% organic base), Capgemini (organic vs M&A mix), DSV (AI savings are a 2030 story, unproven).
- **Avoid confusing re-basing with upgrading:** Honeywell Technologies and Stellantis both presented new frameworks off restructured or depressed starting points.
- **Negative read-across:** Citigroup — a reminder that a large buyback does not compensate for a deferred returns target.
